# Loadouts (ability sets, HUD modules)

Read this before adding or changing a loadout, or touching how Player or the Hud talk to one. Update it at the end of the task (see `docs/mechanics/README.md`). Name tunables, never their values.

**Scripts:** `player/loadout.gd` (`Loadout` base), `player/grapple_loadout.gd` + `.tscn` (`GrappleLoadout`), `player/swing_step.gd` (`SwingStep`), `player/player.gd` (the loadout API), `hud/loadout_hud.gd` (`LoadoutHud` base), `hud/grapple_hud.gd` + `.tscn` (`GrappleHud`), `hud/hud.gd` (`Hud.SLOTS`, `Hud.mount`, `Hud.set_speed_tag`). The jetpack loadout is in `jet/` (`jet.md`).
**Related:** the grapple's own mechanics are in `grapple.md` and `combat.md`; the jetpack's in `jet.md`; the shared body's in `movement.md`; the HUD layout in `levels_and_hud.md`.

## Intent
The grapple is the core verb, but an alternate ability set (a jetpack with tracking weapons) is being tried as an experiment. A **loadout** is one whole ability set, swapped as a unit, so a new one lives in its own files and never adds branches to Player or the Hud. Two exist: `GrappleLoadout` and `JetLoadout` (the default while the jetpack is being playtested).

## How it works
- **Split:** Player is the shared body: walking, jumping, double jump, bunnyhop, momentum flight, wall run, health, lives, camera, rewind. Everything that belongs to one ability set is in its loadout. The grapple loadout holds `GrappleHook` (with rope, sounds, throw), `StrikeGuidance`, `Melee`, `HitStop`, `TrajectoryPredictor` and `AvoidanceAssist` (both are built on the hook), the swing's movement (`SwingStep`) and the attach burst.
- **Choosing one:** `Player.loadout_scene` (a scene whose root extends `Loadout`). Player makes it a child on ready, right after `Head`, so its parts tick before the body's other children (the order the grapple was tuned with).
- **Frame hooks:** Player calls the loadout at fixed points of `_physics_process`: `begin_frame` (before anything moves), `move` (a movement state of its own, the swing: returning true takes the whole frame; not called during a wall run), `steer_flight` (air help after air steering: the strike or the obstacle assist), `stop_flight_guidance` (each wall-run frame and on landing from momentum flight). Also `set_active`, `go_down`, `game_over`, `rewind_capture`/`rewind_restore` (under `&"loadout"` in the player's snapshot) and `add_playtest_fields`. All default to no-ops.
- **Loadout API:** the loadout changes the body's `velocity` and moves it directly, and uses Player's public methods for the shared state: `start_launch_flight`, `keep_momentum`, `stop_jump_rise`, `refill_double_jump`, `land_into_walk`, `ground_move`, `set_walking`, `get_walls`, `mark_force`. Shared parts (camera, head, FOV, `BodyGravity`, `WallRun`, the hand) are handed to `Loadout.setup`. The loadout reports up with signals: its `playtest_event` is re-emitted by Player.
- **Hand:** `Head/Camera3D/Hand`, an empty `Node3D` socket owned by Player at the camera's origin. A loadout puts what the player holds under it (view model with a `Muzzle` child, effects, an `AnimationPlayer`), so it follows the look, shake, bob and lean. Shots and hook rays still come from the camera; the hand is visual only. Nothing uses it yet.
- **HUD modules:** the Hud is a shell with the shared elements (health, lives, speedometer, minimap, damage flash and numbers, death screen). A loadout's `setup_hud` instantiates its `LoadoutHud` scene, calls `Hud.mount`, then sets the elements up. The shell offers a few calls a module may use, such as `Hud.set_speed_tag` (a short tag under the speedometer's number). A module's direct children are plain `Node` holders named after slots (`Hud.SLOTS`: `Underlay`, `Center`, `Overlay`, the edge groups, `AbilityRow` right of the health bar). Mounting moves each holder's elements into that slot, after what is already there, and frees the holders. The module keeps track of its elements and frees them with itself. Reference elements with `@onready` (resolved before the move).

## Settled
- The split is behaviour-neutral for the grapple: a scripted headless run (swing with reel, launch, double jump, strike with melee hit) gave a byte-identical per-frame trace against the pre-split code. Keep it that way when touching the seam.

## Known issues and parked
- No runtime switching yet: changing loadout means changing `loadout_scene` in `player.tscn` (now `jet/jet_loadout.tscn`; `player/grapple_loadout.tscn` for the grapple). A picker (pause menu or per level) is planned; arena and city must get it the same way.
- Wall run, melee and bunnyhop placement: wall run and bunnyhop are in the shared body; melee is in the grapple loadout. The jetpack keeps wall run (from a hover); it has no melee.
- Hand, when a model arrives: offset it down and right, give its materials the FOV override and z-clip scale (Godot's first-person view-model settings, so it doesn't clip into walls or stretch with `DynamicFov`), and decide whether the grapple rope starts at its `Muzzle`. Procedural sway and recoil would go on the socket, with their numbers in `CameraFeel`.
