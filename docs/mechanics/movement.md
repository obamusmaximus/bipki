# Movement (gravity, momentum, air steering, wall run, obstacle assist)

Read this before working on player movement. Update it at the end of the task (see `docs/mechanics/README.md`). Name tunables, never their values.

**Scripts:** `player/player.gd` (`Player`: ground and air movement, momentum, wiring), `body_gravity.gd`, `steering.gd`, `air_drag.gd`, `soft_speed_limit.gd`, `wall_run.gd`, `avoidance_assist.gd`, `trajectory_predictor.gd`, `camera_lean.gd`, `dynamic_fov.gd`, `walk_bob.gd`, `landing_watcher.gd`, `edge_detector.gd`, `edge_probe.gd`, `footsteps.gd`, `hud/trajectory_arc.gd`, `hud/speed_readout.gd`. Tunables: `PlayerStats` (body, gravity, air and swing steering, wall run, obstacle assist, trajectory prediction, edges), `CameraFeel`, `TrajectoryArcStats`.
**Related:** the grapple is in `grapple.md`.

## Intent
A Titanfall-style momentum feel. The player should feel heavy on foot (short hops, dropping back into the streets quickly) but fly far and fast off the grapple. Momentum is never stolen by input. Assists should be a bit arcade-y and invisible, and back off when the player means it. The speed limits are also level-design constraints: raising top speed shrinks every map (`GAME_DESIGN.md`, Scale).

## Ground rules (movement states)
- **Walking, running and wall running are mutually exclusive with the grapple functions.** The player is in one state at a time: on foot, wall running, swinging or in momentum flight.
- **No wall run while swinging.** A swing that meets a wall just slides along it. Attaching a swing ends a wall run (without its cooldown), and the wall can be latched the moment the swing is released.
- **No walking while swinging.** Landing on the floor after having been airborne in a swing detaches it, and that frame is a walking frame. The one exemption is a swing started while standing, which can walk until the player first leaves the floor.
- **A launch ends a wall run** and must send the player flying. It is nullified by hitting the ground or a wall, never converted into walking speed: walking speed is hard capped (`move_speed`), so landing sets it outright.
- A launch can't target the wall being run on or stood beside, nor the ground plane (`grapple.md`).

## How it works
- **Player gravity:** `BodyGravity` is the player's own gravity (`PlayerStats.gravity`, not the project's), heavier than the project default. It is multiplied by `fall_gravity_scale` while falling. On a swing (`swing_gravity_scale`) and for `launch_gravity_time` after a launch (`launch_gravity_scale`; a launch at an enemy holds it for its whole planned flight, which its aim assumes) only a share of it applies, set so swing feel and launch reach stayed as before. Releasing jump while rising keeps `jump_release_cut` of the upward speed. Jump and double jump are roughly a building-floor hop each. Enemies and thrown things keep project gravity. `TrajectoryPredictor` uses the same `BodyGravity`.
- **Momentum:** after a detach, input doesn't override horizontal velocity and gravity applies, until the player lands (`Player._keeps_momentum`).
- **Air steering:** in the air, WASD turns the velocity toward the input at a maximum turn rate (`air_turn_rate_enabled`; turning it off restores adding sideways speed with a fixed acceleration). Lives in `Steering`, with swing steering.
- **Air drag:** `AirDrag` is a small linear drag on the full velocity while airborne or swinging (`air_drag`, a share of speed lost per second). Unlike the soft limit it acts at every speed, so flight bleeds speed gradually. Runs before the soft limit each frame, so it never fights it. Off on the ground and during a wall run.
- **Soft speed limit:** `SoftSpeedLimit` is a drag on airborne and swinging players (`soft_speed_limit_enabled`). Below `soft_speed_limit` nothing happens. Above it, a drag that grows with the cube of the excess levels the speed off near the slider value instead of clamping it. `soft_speed_limit_strength` sets how tightly. It acts on the full 3D speed, so vertical falls are limited too: a long drop levels off at a terminal speed somewhat above `soft_speed_limit`, where gravity and the drag balance (measured headless: a 98 m drop in the arena levels off at that terminal speed, and a downward kick above the limit is slowed to it). It isn't applied on the ground or while wall running. Wall run has its own cap.
- **Wall run:** a fast player (`wall_run_min_speed`) who grazes a wall at a shallow angle (`wall_run_max_approach_degrees`) latches onto it (`WallRun`) instead of bouncing. W (`move_forward`) must be held to start a run, unless the travel direction is more than `wall_run_no_key_degrees` from the look; accidental runs while flying backwards are intended and need no key. The same rule gates the head-on latch and turning a latch into a run, so a corner can't start a run without W. When a run starts, the view turns `wall_run_look_nudge_degrees` away from the wall over `wall_run_look_nudge_time` so the crosshair isn't left on the wall's edge. Gravity is mostly off. A run never climbs: vertical speed is zeroed on latching and clamped to at most zero each frame, and a wall isn't latched at all while rising faster than `wall_run_max_rise_speed` (a launch up past a wall slides past instead). Wall-run gravity is too weak to bleed upward speed, so any that got in carried the player up the wall at a constant rate. Speed is capped at `wall_run_max_speed`, the hook is released, the double jump restored, footsteps play, and the camera tilts away from the wall with a slight shake. Jumping leaves the wall with a push away from it.
- **Head-on latch:** a head-on wall hit latches (`wall_run_head_on_*`). There's no sideways motion, a slow slide down at `wall_run_head_on_gravity_scale` of gravity, no footsteps, and the double jump isn't refilled. Turning the camera to within `wall_run_head_on_turn_degrees` of the wall's line within `wall_run_head_on_time` starts a normal run that way at the speed the wall was hit with. Otherwise the player lets go, falls at normal gravity, and can't latch onto any wall until landing. Jumping during the latch pushes straight back off the wall at `wall_run_head_on_jump_speed`, with the same upward speed as a ground jump (`jump_speed`). The player faces the wall, so that is directly backward, and the momentum flight takes over.
- **Trajectory prediction:** `TrajectoryPredictor` steps the player forward (along the rope when swinging, a plain arc otherwise) and reports the first world hit. The assist and the HUD arc both read it.
- **Trajectory arc:** the faint blue HUD line (`TrajectoryArc`) along the predicted path while airborne. The parts that pass behind a building, as seen from the camera, are hidden (`TrajectoryPredictor.points_visible`).
- **Obstacle assist:** `AvoidanceAssist` is switched on by a predicted collision of the body with a wall while swinging or flying. Merely passing a wall, or wall running along one, doesn't trigger it. It then steers until the path passes at least `assist_clearance` from that wall (checked with a wide upright cylinder sweep), with a firm sideways push sized to get there in the time left (capped by `assist_max_accel`, deliberately a bit arcade-y). It can lift the player over a roof edge in reach instead. It ignores roof tops and the surface right at the hook's attach point (`assist_anchor_radius`), and backs off when the player steers into the surface on purpose. `assist_strength` 0 turns it off. It is off during a launch at an enemy.
- **Camera feel:** `CameraLean` rolls the camera into sideways movement and away from the wall during a wall run. `DynamicFov` widens the view with speed, with a short kick on launch. `WalkBob` is a subtle bob while walking (separate from shake).
- **Speedometer:** `SpeedReadout` shows the horizontal speed (X and Z only) against `soft_speed_limit`, which limits the full 3D speed (see `levels_and_hud.md`).

## Tried and passed on
- **Mantle:** removed. It waits until building visuals match their hitboxes. The old version is in git history before the commit that removed it (`aeb9028`). `EdgeProbe.find_ledge` and `EdgeDetector.ledge_ahead` (probing each frame, unused) are there to reuse.
- **Head-on hang steered with A and D:** the first head-on version froze the player against the wall, and A or D within the time window built speed along it and turned it into a run. Replaced by the slow slide plus a camera turn, because the user wanted the camera to pick the direction. It also made any jump into a wall hang, so the latch only lasts `wall_run_head_on_time` and then locks out wall latching until landing.
- **Fixed-acceleration air steering:** replaced by the turn rate. The old behaviour is still behind `air_turn_rate_enabled`.
- **Project gravity for the player:** replaced by `BodyGravity`. Wall run gravity scales were lowered at the same time so the absolute wall-run sink stayed the same.
