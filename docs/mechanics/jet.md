# Jetpack loadout (hover, surge, afterburner, fuel, tracking)

Read this before working on the jetpack loadout. Update it at the end of the task (see `docs/mechanics/README.md`). Name tunables, never their values.

**Scripts:** `jet/jet_loadout.gd` + `.tscn` (`JetLoadout`), `jet/jet_motor.gd` (`JetMotor`), `jet/jet_sfx.gd` (`JetSfx`), `jet/tracking.gd` (`Tracking`), `jet/scanner_shape.gd` (`ScannerShape`), `jet/hud/jet_hud.gd` + `.tscn` (`JetHud`), `jet/hud/fuel_gauge.gd`, `jet/hud/jet_ability_bar.gd`, `jet/hud/jet_crosshair.gd`, `jet/hud/scanner_frame.gd`, `jet/hud/track_markers.gd`, `player/synth.gd` (`Synth.make_rumble`). Tunables: `JetStats` and `TrackingStats` (tuning panel tab "Jet").
**Related:** how a loadout plugs into Player and the Hud is in `loadouts.md`; the shared body (gravity, momentum flight, wall run, soft speed limit) in `movement.md`.

## Intent
An experimental alternative to the grapple: a free-flying jetpack character, faster than the grapple kit at full burn, with tracking weapons to come. The kit is about handling many targets at once and carries more firepower than the grapple kit. For now it is the default loadout (`Player.loadout_scene`) so it can be playtested.
- **Weapon rules (decided):** no plain gun: every weapon has a mechanic of its own. The micro weapons (used in hover and surge) will draw on the fuel tank, so firepower costs mobility, and since most enemies punish standing still, an empty tank is a danger. That pushes the player to cover and wall run, so levels must work for a grounded player as well as a flying one. The micro weapons are not designed yet. The afterburner's weapon, bombardment, is designed: `bombardment.md`.

## How it works
- **One key:** the `jet` input action (Shift, which the grapple's `reel` also uses). A press shorter than `surge_hold_time` toggles hover when released. Holding it longer with a movement key surges. Held longer without one, nothing happens and releasing doesn't toggle hover. `JetMotor.tick` reads it at the start of the frame (`Loadout.begin_frame`).
- **Hover:** a toggle. In the air the player's air gravity is scaled by `hover_gravity_scale` and the vertical speed settles (`hover_vertical_damping`), and WASD drives the horizontal speed toward `hover_strafe_speed` at `hover_strafe_accel`. That braking means momentum doesn't carry into a hover, except the afterburner's (below). No double jump while hovering (the motor takes the air frames, `Loadout.move`). On the floor Player walks and jumps as usual, and a jump rises into the hover. A hover can latch a wall run like any flight (wall run is in the shared body), and a wall run always switches hover off: leaving the wall is momentum flight until a tap switches hover back on. Switching hover off in the air hands the body to Player's momentum flight. A faint hum loops while hovering in the air.
- **Surge:** held past `surge_hold_time` with WASD: the velocity is set at once to `surge_speed` along the keys relative to the look, pitch included (W flies where the camera points). It then follows the look and keys at `surge_steer_accel`. No gravity, no wall run, and starting one ends a wall run. It ends when the key is released, the movement keys are let go, or the tank runs dry. A surge that ends with hover off hands its momentum to Player's momentum flight. With hover on, the hover brakes it to its strafe speed.
- **Afterburner:** after `afterburner_delay` of surging with only forward held (W, not S), it lights and stays lit until thrust ends. Its thrust (`afterburner_accel`) pushes against a drag that grows with the square of the speed, so the speed levels off softly at `afterburner_speed`, above the shared soft speed limit (thrust frames skip it). Its momentum carries always: into momentum flight, or into a hover, which flies on as momentum flight (air steering, air drag, the soft speed limit) until it has slowed to the strafe speed. Landing ends that carry. `JetLoadout.abilities_locked()` is true while it burns; weapons check it, bombardment excepted (`bombardment.md`).
- **Fuel:** a tank of `fuel_seconds` of thrust. It refills over `fuel_refill_time` once thrust has been off for `fuel_refill_delay`. A tank that runs dry ends the thrust, and the key must be pressed again to thrust after it refills.
- **Speed feedback:** the shared dynamic FOV and speed streaks follow the velocity, with an extra FOV kick (`surge_fov_kick`) when a surge starts and when the afterburner lights. While thrusting, a rushing roar loops over a bass rumble (`Synth.make_rumble`: a low tone and its octave under deep, slowly churning noise), both pitched up for the afterburner, the bass less. A surge starts with an ignition whoosh over a low thump. Levels: `volume_db`, then `thrust_quieter_db` for the roar and ignition and `thrust_bass_db` for the rumble and thump relative to them.
- **HUD (`JetHud`):** a placeholder ring crosshair (centre), the fuel gauge on the right edge (shown while the tank isn't full), the hover (H) and surge (S) indicators right of the health bar (`JetAbilityBar`, built on the grapple's `AbilityIndicator`), and "A/B" under the speedometer while the afterburner burns (`Hud.set_speed_tag`).
- **Ability indicators:** H is orange while hover is on. S shows the hold toward a surge as an orange outline growing clockwise from the top middle (full once held `surge_hold_time`), then is orange while thrusting (surge or afterburner).
- **Tracking:** `Tracking` keeps the set of tracked enemies the weapons will aim at, and reports changes with `tracks_changed`. It works automatically, with no key, in game time.
  - **Scanner:** a pyramid in front of the view. On screen it is a rectangle `scanner_share` of the screen in the middle, so it scales with the FOV (`DynamicFov` included). Its edges bow by `frame_edge_curve` (the corners stay put). `ScannerShape` holds the shape, and both the scanner test and the HUD frame use it, so the frame is exact. An enemy that stays in it, within `scanner_range` and in sight (a ray from the camera that hits no World), for `scanner_pickup_time` is tracked for `track_time`. Leaving it before then starts the pickup over. A tracked enemy in the scanner has its track refreshed to at least `track_time`. The scanner holds up to `max_tracks`.
  - **Scouting:** while the afterburner burns, every enemy in sight within `scout_range`, in any direction, is tracked at once for `scout_track_time`. Scouting isn't limited by `max_tracks`, but the scanner adds nothing while that many or more are tracked.
  - A track ends when its time runs out or its enemy dies.
- **Tracking HUD:** a thin blue frame shows the scanner (`ScannerFrame`). Thin red rectangles mark tracked enemies (`TrackMarkers`), `marker_world_size` across in the world (at least `marker_min_px`). They blink at `marker_blink_rate` during the last `marker_blink_time` of a track. Both are full screen in the Hud's `Underlay`, unscaled by the HUD scale, because they match the 3D view.
- **Rewind:** hover, fuel, the refill wait and the afterburner carry are saved. Thrust isn't: a death stops it, and a key still held after the restore does nothing until it is pressed again. Tracks aren't saved: the player comes back with none.

## Known issues and parked
- **Placeholders to tune:** `fuel_seconds` (the user may lower it); the refill rule (anywhere, after a delay), `hover_vertical_damping` and `scanner_range` were picked without a brief.
- Once the afterburner ends, momentum flight's soft speed limit pulls its speed back down to `soft_speed_limit` within a second or so, a hover's carry included. If the afterburner's speed should last longer, the carry needs its own limit.
- The speedometer dial is scaled to `soft_speed_limit`, so the afterburner pins it at the top.
- Tracking: enemies tracked behind the camera (scouting) have no marker, and there is no sign of a pickup in progress. Edge arrows and a filling marker are candidates.
- Next: bombardment (build order in `bombardment.md`; the user directs the weapon build step by step), the micro weapons (undecided; fuel as ammo, no plain gun), then a loadout picker (`loadouts.md`). Glossary must avoid "enemy lock" (the grapple's snap, `combat.md`).
